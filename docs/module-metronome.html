metronome

Metronome (2.11, free time signatures 2.12, silences and tempo ramp 2.14): settings, validation and the sounds of one cycle. Pure functions, tested under Node. The audio engine is metro-engine, the interface ui/metronome.

Time signature, pulses and groups

Any time signature num/den: 1 to 32 over 2, 4, 8 or 16. The bar has num pulses (one per unit of the bottom number). In /8 and /16, the pulses are gathered into groups (7/8 as 2+2+3, 15/16 as 4+4+4+3): the first pulse of each group is accented by default. In /2 and /4, each pulse is a beat and may be subdivided.

Levels

Every pulse, and every subdivision click, has a level from 0 (silent) to 4 (full). A muted cell remembers its level: it is stored negative (-3 = muted, comes back at 3), so switching it back on restores it.

Tempo

The tempo counts pulses (♪ = 280 in 7/8), or groups when all groups have the same length and that length is a note value (♩. = 80 in 6/8).

One cycle, or a stream of bars

Usually the engine plays one pre-rendered cycle in a loop: one bar, or on + off bars when whole bars are silenced (cycleEvents). When every bar may differ (tempo ramp, random or growing silences), it renders the bars a little ahead, chunk after chunk, from planBar.

Jazz modes

They replace the pulse levels while they are on:

  • 2 and 4: only pulses 2 and 4 click (even number of beats, /2 or /4);
  • once per bar: a single pulse clicks (the first by default). For "every other bar", combine it with the silences (1 bar with, 1 without).

Silences and tempo ramp (2.14)

  • Silences, to work on the inner tempo: whole bars (2 with click, 2 without…), pulses silenced at random (10 to 90 %), or random silences that grow over the session. The lights go on during the silence.
  • Tempo ramp: from the tempo of the main page to a target, by steps (+4 every 4 bars…); at the end, stay, start again or stop.
