placements

Rhythm practice: placements (since 2.3, every time signature since 2.8). Pure functions, shared with the exercise generator (exercise).

A measure is split into eighth-note positions. In 4/4:

position: 0  1  2  3  4  5  6  7
count:    1  &  2  &  3  &  4  &

Eighth notes are arranged in groups of 2 or 3 (one beat): pairs in x/4, groups of 3 in 6/8, 9/8, 12/8, and a mix in 5/8 or 7/8 (see METERS). For n notes, every way of choosing n positions is generated (combinations), each placement is written group by group (placementItems), then grouped into series by position of the first note (placementSeries).

Writing rules (eighth notes)

Group of 2 (one beat in x/4):

eighths written as
x. quarter note
.x eighth rest + eighth note
xx two eighths (beamed)
.. quarter rest

Group of 3 (one beat of compound time):

eighths written as
x.. dotted quarter note
.x. eighth rest, eighth note, eighth rest
..x quarter rest, eighth note
xx. eighth note, quarter note
x.x quarter note, eighth note
.xx eighth rest, two eighths
xxx three eighths
... dotted quarter rest

A note never spills over into the next group (no syncopation). Then the rests of two neighboring empty beats are merged (half rest, or dotted half rest in compound time):

  • in 3 beats (3/4, 9/8): any pair;
  • otherwise, only a pair starting on an odd beat (1-2, 3-4): in 4/4, the middle of the measure must stay visible, never 2-3;
  • never in odd meters (5/8, 7/8): each group stays readable.
